Understanding ML Metrics

Accuracy

Ratio of correct predictions to total observations. Measures general classifier correctness.

Precision

Percentage of positive predictions that are correct. Protects against false positive diagnoses.

Recall (Sensitivity)

Percentage of actual positives correctly identified. Vital in medicine so no diabetic cases are missed.

F1 Score

Harmonic mean of Precision and Recall. Evaluates balanced classifier performance.
