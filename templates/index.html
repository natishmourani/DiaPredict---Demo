Diagnostic Indicators

Pregnancies

Number of pregnancies. Helps correlate endocrine changes with insulin response.

Glucose

Plasma glucose concentration. The key clinical metric for evaluating insulin production.

Blood Pressure

Diastolic blood pressure (mm Hg). Used to check cardiovascular symptoms.

Skin Thickness

Triceps skin fold thickness (mm). Correlates skin fold depth with obesity indicators.

Insulin

2-Hour serum insulin (mu U/ml). Direct measure of blood glucose balancing hormone.

BMI

Body mass index (weight/height²). Principal indicator of weight-related health risks.

DPF

Diabetes Pedigree Function. Formulates hereditary risk factors from family history.

Age

Age of the patient. Risk ranges generally increase with advancing years.
